    - Room guard (18.10.2026, every version): each frame the game writes its GPU primitives into one of
      two buffers of 103,480 bytes. The object, sprite and effect routines check the end of the buffer;
      the room geometry routine does not, so in very busy views (Highland Fling, side path room 2 ->
      room 3: 19-26 rooms, about 4,000 faces) it ran past the end into the buffer being displayed and
      the memory behind it (broken polygons, hanging picture, crash at the next loading screen; the
      single crash under the bridge on 15.10.2026 was the same, at about 3,500 faces). Measured live in
      DuckStation: the game's own high-water mark (gp+0x23A0, in 52-byte units) stood at 1990 of 1990.
      bin/patches/<version>/roomguard.ips (tools/make_roomguard_ips.py) redirects the call that draws
      each room piece to a 12-instruction routine that skips the piece when less than 8 KB are left

      limit the farthest pieces, already in the black fog, are left out. Tested with the open side
      path: 200-325 pieces left out per second there, no errors, no crash, every level flown through.
      The routine sits in an unused game function found on the executable with all other patches
      applied (make_reunionview_ips.unused_routines), so it never overlaps the draw distance patch.

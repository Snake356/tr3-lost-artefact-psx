================================================================================
  TOMB RAIDER III: THE LOST ARTEFACT - PS1
  Changes, known issues and fixed bugs
================================================================================


  This file lists what is different from the PC version and why, the known
  issues that are left as they are, and the bugs that were fixed.

  Found something new? Report it on the project page:

     https://github.com/Snake356/tr3-lost-artefact-psx/issues

  The builder's "Report a problem" button opens a report that is already
  filled in with your disc version and options. Please add the level, the
  place (room number from TRView if you can) and what happens. A crash or
  freeze matters most: please say what you did just before it.

  VERSIONS
================================================================================

  1.0.9  Hotfix: in a crawlspace, Jump at the edge from the crouch flips
         Lara out without Duck held, as in TRX (Duck was needed even
         where the low ceiling keeps her down). The crouch roll follows
         TRX too: Sprint in the crawl takes Lara into the crouch and she
         rolls, and there is no roll with Draw held or in a swamp.
         Savegames of 1.0.8 keep working.

  1.0.8  Update: new moves in every disc, as in TRX (see EXTRAS in the
         README). A controlled drop: step off a high edge with Action held
         and the hands free, and Lara turns round and catches the edge.
         Ledge jumps: while hanging, Jump with Forward jumps up, Jump with
         Back jumps backwards. Corner shimmying: while hanging from a ledge,
         Left or Right where the ledge ends takes Lara round an outer or an
         inner corner. Climbing round corners: on a climbable wall, Left or
         Right where the wall ends does the same. Lara's home has the new
         moves too. The new moves use the sounds TRX gives them.

         More level memory is free: Lara's animations are stored more
         compactly. Highland Fling has about 33.9 KB free now (1.0.7: about
         2.4 KB). The code of all the extras takes 6.5 KB of the level memory
         (7.3 KB on the US discs).

         IMPORTANT: savegames from 1.0.7 and older do not work with 1.0.8,
         because Lara's animations are numbered differently now. Start the
         level again from the level select, or start a new game.

         Fixes: the mist of Highland Fling and Willard's Lair no longer
         disappears under the floor it lies on, and the green mist of
         Reunion and It's a Madhouse! no longer shows steps at its edges
         (floor and walls were drawn over it). Crawlspaces and steep
         slopes no longer block Lara wrongly in the crawl and crouch. The
         end of a speech no longer stops the picture. Sprinting up a step

         The final statistics play track 14. The game speed is even again.
         The crouch roll checks the floor ahead, and Lara's right arm no
         longer jumps at its end. Under water Draw takes the harpoon gun.
         The diver in Sleeping with the Fishes disappears again after its

         other objects are copied to the neighbouring rooms as TRX does,
         so fewer of them are cut off. The passport's spine has its
         colour in every level, and the footsteps on 16 floor squares of
         It's a Madhouse! are right.

  1.0.7  Update: the flip out of a crawlspace needs only Jump now (Walk
         no longer), as in TRX: at the edge in the crawl, also right
         when Lara stops after crawling forward, or from the crouch with

  1.0.5  Hotfix: after the flip out of a crawlspace Lara could not crawl

  1.0.4  Hotfix: the flip out of a crawlspace (Walk + Jump at the edge)

  1.0.3  New in every disc: a forward roll out of the crouch (Sprint),

         3.8 KB in every level.

         chosen with "New Game"

================================================================================
  ON PURPOSE: DIFFERENT FROM THE PC VERSION
================================================================================

  The PlayStation has 2 MB of main memory and 512 KB of sound memory. Some
  changes were needed to make the levels fit and run.

  SLEEPING WITH THE FISHES

  * The level is split into two parts, each with its own loading screen.
    -> In one piece it is too big for the PS1: as soon as several enemies
       became active at once, the game ran out of memory and crashed.
       (FindingTheLost found this and made the split.)

  * Each part is the whole level file with the other part taken out.
    Part 1 drops the rooms, items, models, lights and floor data of
    Part 2, and Part 2 drops those of Part 1. That is what frees the
    memory. Some things had to stay or be handled with care:
    -> Part 1 keeps the geometry of one room of Part 2 (room 54). Without
       it, the green acid in the camera shot at the very start of the
       level shows broken textures. This was tested twice with the same
       result, so the room stays.
    -> Part 1 also keeps room 13 of Part 2, so that the view at the end of
       room 44 does not end in nothing.
    -> Part 1 ends with a new level end in room 44, where Part 2 begins.
    -> Part 2 keeps three rooms of Part 1 (rooms 26, 27 and 28), so that
       the last aquarium does not look black and empty. What lies outside
       the glass of the tank with the Uzis is still not visible.
    -> Two SLINC diving bells in Part 2 are turned so that their missing
       back (which lay in Part 1) cannot be seen.
    -> Making the left-out rooms smaller as well was tried and dropped:
       it broke textures and caused random freezes.
    -> Part 2 drops two triggers it does not need: one at the end of room
       44 that cleared bodies and one at the start of room 13 that
       switched off all triggers.

  * The yellow submarine standing before the Desert Eagle secret is left
    out.
    -> The PS1 draws this large model wrongly: its picture jumps to the
       room in front while it still blocks the way, and moving it did not
       help (found by FindingTheLost). It is one of the largest models in
       the level and reaches over the edge of its room. The level plays
       the same without it.

  HIGHLAND FLING

  * Rooms are drawn up to 14 blocks away instead of 20, with the
    original fog. Enemies, items and other objects are still drawn up to
    20 blocks, as in the original game.
    -> For a smoother frame rate in this large outdoor level.

  REUNION

  * The punks no longer carry burning clubs.
    -> All ten burning at once was too much for the PS1. A warm glow around
       them makes up for it.

  * Rooms are drawn up to 12 blocks away instead of 20, with closer fog
    (black at 16 blocks); objects are still drawn up to 20 blocks. Two
    rooms got some extra light.

  SOUNDS

  The PS1 keeps all sounds of a level in its own 512 KB of sound memory.
  The original Tomb Raider III levels use between 439 and 499 KB of it.
  The Lost Artefact levels have more sounds than fit, so a few that are
  rarely heard or have other variants are silent. Most of this was already
  done in b122251's conversion; it was checked again for this release.
  A silent sound is now a short real silence; before, an empty sample could
  loop and take up a sound channel.

  * Shakespeare Cliff: the push switch, the Uzi stop and shotgun shell
    sounds, the reservoir flush, a small fan, the attack of the winged
    mutant, two of the generator and two of the squeak variants, four of
    Lara's wet-feet steps and her wading, a monkey roll and the prisoner's
    sounds.
    -> The bazooka shot and the quad bike hitting a wall or landing after
       a jump can be heard again; the level now uses about 490 KB of sound
       memory. The quad bike's engine and all its other sounds are there.
       The monkey roll and the prisoner's sounds are silent instead: the
       level never uses them (there is no prisoner in it).

  * Willard's Lair: the crocodile's attack and death, the blowpipe, metal
    shutters breaking, a wooden gate, the portcullis going up and down,
    the wall blades, a small subway door, a light water sound, a water
    loop, one generator variant, the Uzi stop, and four of Lara's
    wet-feet steps and her wading.

  * Sleeping with the Fishes (both parts): a jet flying by, a generic
    swoosh, a light water sound, Lara's death on spikes, metal shutters
    breaking, one mud footstep variant, four of Lara's wet-feet steps and
    her wading, and the security guard's sounds (there is no guard in
    these levels).

  * It's a Madhouse!: Lara using a key, the portcullis going up and down,
    a trapdoor closing and small metal shutters.

  * Highland Fling and Reunion: nothing silent.

  SAVE CRYSTALS

  * The PS1 version saves with save crystals, like the original Tomb
    Raider III on the PS1. They were placed in every level and checked
    by hand.

  JAPANESE AND ASIAN DISC 1

  * Easier levels: more pickups and save crystals, fewer traps, and the
    grenade launcher already in Highland Fling. This is how the original
    Japanese Tomb Raider III differs from the western one. The Lost
    Artefact came out in Japan only on the PC, with the same levels as the
    western version, so these levels were adapted by hand for this
    project, following the original Japanese Tomb Raider III.
  * As in the Japanese Tomb Raider III, the game is saved from a save
    crystal in the inventory, and the textures are brighter.

  START AND END

  * The game starts like the original Tomb Raider III on the PS1: the
    language's loading picture and the Eidos logo video (Enix logo on the
    Japanese discs).
  * The end credits show ten pictures: the Lost Artefact credits, the
    PlayStation edition credit and b122251's farewell picture.


================================================================================
  KNOWN ISSUES
================================================================================

  * In large or open areas the frame rate drops to about 15-20 frames per
    second. The original Tomb Raider III on the PS1 does the same in many
    places.

  * Sleeping with the Fishes Part 2: the two SLINC capsules hanging in the
    open water look very dark on the PS1.

  * Not a bug: the Hand of Rathmore (It's a Madhouse!), the Circuit Bulbs
    and the Mutant Samples (Sleeping with the Fishes Part 2) are drawn
    see-through and are hard to see in the dark. The original Tomb Raider
    III on the PS1 draws such items the same way. Light a flare.

  * Formerly reported: the quad bike's sound in Shakespeare Cliff glitched
    now and then. It was not seen any more in the latest tests.

    above the arena with an explosion (a rocket or a grenade close to the
    box) or a harpoon.


================================================================================
  FIXED BUGS
================================================================================

  Every fix applies to all disc versions (USA, Europe, Japan, Asia, both
  discs, every revision) unless a version is named. Small things were
  fixed too, so that the PS1 version is as clean and complete as possible.

  Each entry says what was wrong, why, and what was done. "(FTL)" marks
  fixes by FindingTheLost, "(found by FTL)" bugs he found and listed that
  were fixed later; all others were found and fixed in this continuation
  of the project. b122251's own groundwork (moving enemy code to free slots, the
  savegame name, the sky colour, the loading bar and more) is not listed,
  because it made the port possible in the first place.


  HIGHLAND FLING
  --------------

  * The picture broke up and froze when looking from the side path
    between rooms 2 and 3 into room 3, and the game crashed at the next
    loading screen. It could also happen, very rarely, under the bridge.
    Why: the game collects everything it draws in a frame in a buffer for
    the graphics chip. Objects and effects stop when it is full, but the
    room geometry did not: in these views it wrote past the end of the
    buffer, into the picture being shown and into the memory behind it.
    Fix: a piece of room geometry is now only drawn while the buffer still
    has room for it. At that limit the farthest pieces, already deep in
    the black distance fog, are left out for that frame. This guard works
    in every level and every disc version. The side path, closed with a
    wall in version 1.0, is open again as on the PC. The camera of the
    short shot of the guard in room 4 keeps the place it had in 1.0, at
    the edge of room 4: in room 2 it would stand right in the busiest
    view of the level.

  * The Loch Ness monster did not show up in its camera shot (when you
    pick up the shotgun on the bridge).
    Why: the shorter view distance of this level (14 blocks) was applied
    to everything, objects included, and the monster swims further out
    on the loch, so it was left out although its room was drawn.
    Fix: the shorter distance applies to the rooms only; objects are
    drawn up to 20 blocks as in the original game. Reunion (12 blocks)
    is set the same way.

  * The mist of the rooms disappeared under the floor it lay on.
    Fix: the mist is drawn nearer, so it stays visible over that floor.
    Its strength is set per room (the same fix is in WILLARD'S LAIR).

  * The helicopter, a far looping sound, could keep playing until the
    inventory was opened.
    Fix: a sound that takes over a busy voice really restarts it.

  * You could walk into the stone pillar in room 49, and reach into it
    from the room above (room 20).
    Why: on the PC these two spots belong to a third room (room 21) in
    which the pillar is solid. To save memory, the PS1 version leaves room
    21 out. b122251 had already closed the neighbouring spots for the same
    reason, but these two were missed.
    Fix: both spots are now solid walls. All seven levels were checked for
    other places of this kind: there are none.



  WILLARD'S LAIR
  --------------

  * (FTL) When the Cairn Key was inserted, the camera looked at the wrong
    place.
    Fix: the camera now shows the right spot.

  * The mist disappeared under the floor it lay on.
    Fix: as in HIGHLAND FLING, the mist is drawn nearer and its strength
    is set per room.


  SLEEPING WITH THE FISHES
  ------------------------

  * (FTL) The level crashed the game once several enemies became active.
    Why: an enemy only takes memory when it wakes up. In this large level,
    several enemies waking up at once used up the PS1's 2 MB and the game
    crashed. Making the rooms simpler does not help against that.
    Fix: the level is split into Part 1 and Part 2, each of which fits on
    its own, with its own loading screen. Part 2 got a save crystal near
    the start.

  * Broken, stretched floor geometry in rooms 14, 44 and 53.
    Why: these rooms were taller than the PS1 room format allows (31
    steps of height); the highest points were cut off and folded down.
    An earlier attempt only moved the problem to other points.
    Fix: the affected points belong to a decorative floor far below the
    real one and were raised a little. Now no point is cut off in any of
    the 70 level files.

  * The animated diver left stretched polygons behind and then lay frozen
    in his last pose.
    Why: his animation jumps to a frame that does not exist. This is a bug
    in the original game data (the original Tomb Raider III level Lud's
    Gate has it too).
    Fix: the jump goes to the right frame, and the diver disappears when
    his animation is over instead of lying there.

  * The diver lay frozen again after his animation. Since 1.0.3 the new
    Lara animations had shifted his animation number.
    Fix: his animation number is set again, so he disappears at the end.

  * A cage in Part 2 was faulty and the area around it was dark.
    Fix: the cage is repaired and the area gets a cool light.


  IT'S A MADHOUSE!
  ----------------

  * (FTL) A vulture got stuck in a wall (as on the PC).
    Fix: it was moved a little and set lower, so it flies free.

  * (found by FTL) An invisible item was already in Lara's inventory at
    the start of the level: a "Hand of Rathmore" with no model and no
    texture, an empty slot.
    Why: at the end of Sleeping with the Fishes Part 2 Lara picks up the
    Hand of Rathmore. Tomb Raider III puts the icons of the artefacts
    collected so far back into the inventory at the start of every level
    (in the original game for the artefacts of the earlier levels). It's a
    Madhouse! does not load a model for the Hand, so the item showed up
    empty.
    Fix: the artefact icons are no longer put back at the start of a
    level.

  * (found by FTL) The save crystals were drawn too small.
    Why: they used the small crystal model.
    Fix: they now use the big crystal model like in all other levels.

  * Two wall faces in room 97 showed the wrong texture.
    Fix: they now have the right one (the Japanese version already had
    it).

  * Sixteen floor squares gave the wrong footstep sound.
    Fix: they give the footstep sound of their material.

  * The green mist showed steps at its edges, where floor and walls were
    drawn over it.
    Fix: the mist is drawn a little nearer (the same fix as in HIGHLAND
    FLING, at the lowest strength).


  REUNION
  -------

  Reunion takes the place of the Tomb Raider III level Madubu Gorge in the
  game's level list (level 7). Some game code checks the level number and
  behaves specially for the level that originally had that number. That
  caused the first two bugs:

  * When Lara touched the green acid, she did not burn. She played the
    wrong reaction meant for the rapids of Madubu Gorge (a drowning-like
    reaction, tinted green), and the game could crash.
    Why: the code for deadly surfaces treats level 7 as Madubu Gorge and
    its rapids.
    Fix: that special case no longer applies, so Lara burns as on the PC.

  * Sophia could not be defeated: destroying the fuse box above the arena
    had no effect.
    Why: in Tomb Raider III, Sophia only becomes vulnerable when the fuse
    box is destroyed, and the fuse box code checks for one fixed level
    number from Tomb Raider III. Reunion has a different number, so the
    check never matched.
    Fix: the fuse box code now checks for Reunion's number. Destroying the
    box (a rocket or grenade close to it, or a harpoon) defeats Sophia.

  * A blue, noisy band showed below the sky when looking out over the
    fence.
    Why: the sky dome sat lower than usual, and its lower part is never
    drawn on the PS1, so the empty background showed through.
    Fix: the dome is back at its normal height and closed at the bottom
    with a black ring.

  * Leftovers from It's a Madhouse! were still in an unreachable room
    (room 51): a flare box and triggers for a secret, music and "clear
    bodies".
    Fix: removed, so that nothing of the other level is left in Reunion.

  * The punks' glow and their room light were in the wrong colour.
    Fix: both take the colour of the light nearest to them in each room.

  * The green mist showed steps at its edges, where floor and walls were
    drawn over it.
    Fix: the mist is drawn a little nearer (the same fix as in HIGHLAND
    FLING, at the lowest strength).

  * (FTL) A save crystal was added in the room with the lever just before
    Sophia (later moved, see "All levels").


  ALL LEVELS
  ----------

  * Crawl and crouch: a crawlspace under an opening counted as a wall, and
    a steep slope ahead blocked Lara even above her feet.
    Fix: the front check starts in Lara's own room, and a steep slope is a
    wall only above her feet, as in TR4 and TR5.

  * The end of a speech stopped the picture for a third of a second.
    Fix: it no longer does; the first speech of a level costs no frame.

  * Sprinting up a step stopped the sprint.
    Fix: the sprint is kept, as in TRX.

  * A diver killed by a rocket stayed visible, and dead divers and
    alligators stayed in view while the other bodies disappear.
    Fix: a killed orca, diver or alligator stays gone, and dead divers and
    alligators disappear out of sight like the other bodies.

  * The final statistics played the wrong music.
    Fix: track 14, as in TRX.

  * The game speed was uneven (the frame counter was cleared at the end of
    each frame). Fix: the speed is even.

  * The crouch roll could run into a wall, up a high step or over an edge,
    and Lara's right arm jumped at its end.
    Fix: the roll checks the floor ahead, as in TRX, and the arm leads
    smoothly into the crouch.

  * Under water Draw took the wrong gun.
    Fix: it takes the harpoon gun, and out of the water the gun used
    before, as in TRX.

  * Statues and other static objects were cut off more often than
    needed at the edge of their room.
    Fix: they are copied to the neighbouring rooms in the way TRX does;
    those that vanish with a room change are not copied. Depending on
    the view some are still cut off, as in the original game.

  * The passport's spine in the inventory ring was black in some levels.
    Fix: it has its colour in every level.

  * Save crystals: (FTL) added crystals in Sleeping with the Fishes Part 2
    and It's a Madhouse!. Later crystals were placed in all levels, then
    checked and adjusted by hand in every level (some added, moved or
    removed). Two crystals in the
    Japanese Highland Fling could be picked up like items; fixed.

  * (FTL) The number of secrets: Sleeping with the Fishes Part 1 and Part
    2 and It's a Madhouse! showed the wrong number of secrets, and the
    final statistics screen the wrong total. Both are correct now.

  * (FTL) Loading screens were cut at the top and bottom, as on the PC
    Gold version. They were rebuilt in the right proportions, like the
    original Tomb Raider III loading screens on the PS1. All title, legal,
    credit and loading pictures are finished.

  * Lara's head had two wrongly textured triangles.
    Fix: they have a new skin texture in every level. Lara's home (the
    original Tomb Raider III level from your disc) had the same fault: a
    yellow band where the nose should be. It gets the same skin texture
    when the disc is built.

  * Lara's home: its music track is "none", which did not stop the music
    of the level played before; it played on, and came back after every
    speech. Fix: the music stops when Lara's home starts.

  * End of the game: the statistics come from Tomb Raider III and set
    the next level to its bonus level (20) when every secret was found;
    The Lost Artefact has none, so that savegame pointed to a level that
    does not exist. Fix: the savegame after the credits always starts in
    the first level. It also offered only the New Game+ savegame, so a
    normal game last saved before Reunion could not reach Reunion when
    loaded; the normal savegame is offered first now, with every level
    reached.

  * PAL discs: the title picture had black borders at the top and the
    bottom, as the NTSC one. Fix: it fills the whole picture, as Tomb
    Raider III's PAL title does (the loading pictures keep their borders,
    as in the original).

  * Title picture (all discs): the PC title it is made from has a dark
    stripe at the right edge, a plain blue corner at the bottom left and
    a lighter blue patch beside Lara's arm. Fix: retouched from the PC
    picture (the frost pattern continues into the corner, the edge and the
    patch take the colour around them).

  * Load Game with both savegames on the memory card (normal and New
    Game+): the button hints ("Select", "Go Back") flickered when the page
    opened and were gone after the choice until another page (level
    select, Lara's home) was opened; in the inventory "Select" was lost
    for good after a load left with "Continue" and a second "Load Game".
    Fix: every text the page removes before reading the memory card (the
    hints, the page title, the item name, the heading) and the list's own
    texts are removed right before the card is read, as the game does it
    without the choice.

  * Load Game in a level asked for the mode as well (New Game or New
    Game+), so a wrong answer left the game mode running. Now it asks only
